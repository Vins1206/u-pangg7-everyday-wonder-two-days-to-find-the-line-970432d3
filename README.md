A make.fun entry for [Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line](https://make.fun/c/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3): one sentence, at most 200 characters, that makes a small everyday moment strange and new.

## The sentence

> The last ice cube in my glass cracked like a knuckle, and suddenly the drink was listening back.

96 characters, one sentence. A kitchen moment — the last ice cube cracking — turned strange by the drink listening back: a fresh angle, a surprise that lands in one read, grounded in something real and specific.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. Pure Python stdlib, no dependencies, deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output.
